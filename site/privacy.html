Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Snapply is a browser extension and online service that helps you fill job applications. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, and your rights under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Snapply is operated by Tolga Oy, a sole trader in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller. Address: Flat 6, 402 Holloway Road, London N7 6PZ, United Kingdom. Contact: hello@snapply.co.uk.

What never reaches our servers

Answers that Snapply classifies as legal declarations (such as right to work, sponsorship, criminal convictions, barring lists and consents), personal or equality information (such as date of birth, ethnicity, disability, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, nationality) and referee details are stored only in your browser’s extension storage on your own device. Documents you add under “My documents” (such as qualification certificates or membership certificates) are also kept only in your browser and are attached to applications only on your own device. Our servers reject them. To match an application form to its job advert, Snapply keeps the text of the last 15 job adverts you opened, also only in your browser; only adverts opened in the last six hours are used. These answers are suggested to you and only filled after you confirm them each time.

AI processing

When you ask for an AI draft or cover letter, the relevant parts of your CV, the job advert and the question are sent to our AI provider, OpenAI, to generate the text. OpenAI processes this data on our behalf, does not use API data to train its models by default, and may process it in the United States; this transfer is protected by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the standard contractual clauses. Nothing is sent to the AI provider unless you press a drafting button.

Who we share data with

Only service providers who process data on our instructions: Fly.io (hosting, London region), OpenAI (AI drafting), Stripe (payments) and Google (sign-in). We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Deleting your account (Snapply panel → Delete account, or by emailing us) removes your profile, CV and saved answers from our servers immediately; backups are overwritten within 7 days. Stripe keeps payment records as required by law. Data in your browser is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear its storage.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each account’s data is stored separately and can only be accessed with that account’s signed session.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent where it applies. Email hello@snapply.co.uk; we reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the extension.
