Questions

Does Snapply submit applications for me?

No. It fills and checks the form; you review every page and press submit yourself.

Will it make things up?

No. Facts come from your CV. If something is not in your CV, Snapply asks you instead of guessing, and AI drafts are checked against your CV before you see them.

Which sites does it work on?

Workday, Greenhouse and NHS Jobs / TRAC are fully supported; Lever, Ashby and CIPHR are detected and partly supported, with more coming.

Does it work on a tablet or phone?

Not yet — Snapply runs in Chrome on a computer. An iPad version is planned.

How do I cancel?

In the Snapply panel: Manage subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
