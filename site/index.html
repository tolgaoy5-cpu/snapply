How it works

Your CV in. Applications out.

The same twenty questions on every site. Snapply answers the ones it knows and asks you only once for the rest.

PDF or Word. Snapply builds your profile — roles, dates, education, skills — and you check it before anything is filled.

Press “Fill this page”

Work history, education, dates, languages and contact details, committed so the site actually accepts them.

Review and submit

Check every page, answer anything new once, and press submit yourself. Snapply never submits for you.

Remembers for you

Answer once. Reused everywhere.

A new question appears in the Snapply panel with the site's own options. Answer it once — the same or a reworded question on any other site is filled for you next time.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Snapply submit applications for me?

No. It fills and checks each page; you review everything and press submit yourself.

Will it make things up?

No. Facts come from your CV. If something is not in your CV, Snapply asks you instead of guessing, and AI drafts are checked against your CV before you see them.

What counts as an application?

One job you apply to. All the pages and steps of that application count once, however many times you press “Fill this page”.

Which sites does it work on?

Workday, Greenhouse, NHS Jobs / TRAC, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Teamtailor and TalentLink (used by many universities and councils) are tested regularly. On other application forms Snapply recognises the fields it can and uses AI to understand unfamiliar ones — and if a page does not fill properly, one click in the panel tells us so we can fix it.

Do I buy Pro here or in the extension?

Either. Press “Get Pro” above and sign in with the same Google account you use in the extension — your plan appears there straight away.

Does it work on a tablet or phone?

Not yet — Snapply runs in Chrome on a computer. An iPad version is planned.

How do I cancel?

From the Snapply panel (Manage subscription) or by signing in here. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Which Pro plan should I choose?

All plans include the same features. A week suits one or two urgent applications; three months costs less per day if you are applying steadily. Each plan renews at the same length until you cancel.
