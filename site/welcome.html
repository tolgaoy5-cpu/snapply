Snapply is installed.

Three quick steps and your next application fills itself.

  1. Pin Snapply to your toolbarClick the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Snapply.
  2. Sign in with GoogleOpen Snapply from the toolbar and press “Sign in with Google”. Your plan and saved answers follow your account.
  3. Upload your CV onceMy Profile → Import or replace CV. Check the details it found, then open any job application and press “Fill this page”.
