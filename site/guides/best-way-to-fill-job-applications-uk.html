The fastest way to fill in job applications in the UK

A practical comparison of the ways to stop retyping your CV into every UK application form: browser autofill, a master document and job application autofill extensions.

UK job seekers often fill in dozens of application forms, each asking for the same details, work history and questions. These are the main ways to speed that up, from simplest to most automatic.

1. Browser autofill (free, built in)

Fills name, email, phone and address. Does not fill work history, education, uploads or questions.

2. A master document (free)

One document with every job’s dates and duties, qualifications, referees and your standard answers. Reliable, but you still copy and paste every field.

3. A job application autofill extension

Reads your CV and fills the long parts of the form. Snapply is one built for UK forms: it fills contact details, every job and qualification row (NHS Jobs, TRAC, Workday and more), attaches your CV and reuses answers you gave before. It never submits for you and never fills equality or legal questions on its own. Free for 5 applications a month.

What still needs you

  • Tailoring the supporting statement or cover letter to the advert (cover letter guide).
  • Eligibility and legal declarations.
  • A final check of every page before you submit.
