NHS Jobs application form: a step-by-step guide

How to complete an application on NHS Jobs (jobs.nhs.uk): what each part asks for and how shortlisting works.

NHS Jobs (jobs.nhs.uk) is the main job site for NHS roles in England and Wales. Some adverts are completed on NHS Jobs itself; others send you to the trust’s own system, such as TRAC (see our TRAC guide).

1. Read the job pack first

Download the job description and person specification. Note the essential criteria and which are assessed on the application form. That tells you what your answers must cover.

2. Work through the form

  • Personal details and eligibility: contact details, right to work, and any registration the role needs.
  • Employment history: each job with dates, employer, title and main responsibilities. Most recent first.
  • Education and training: qualifications with dates and grades, and relevant training.
  • References: normally your current or most recent employer, covering a set number of years.
  • Supporting information: the most important part — evidence against the person specification. See how to write it.
  • Declarations and equality monitoring.

3. How shortlisting usually works

Shortlisters normally score applications against the person specification. Clear evidence for each essential criterion matters more than length. Many trusts shortlist without seeing your personal details, so the evidence has to speak for itself.

4. Before you submit

  • Check dates line up and gaps are explained.
  • Make sure the supporting information is tailored to this post, not copied from another.
  • Check the closing date — some adverts close early.
