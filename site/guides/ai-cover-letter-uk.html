Using AI to write a cover letter — without making things up

How to use AI for a UK cover letter safely: give it your real CV and the advert, then check every claim.

AI tools can draft a cover letter in seconds. The risk is that they invent things: a team you never led, a qualification you do not have, numbers that sound impressive but are not yours. Recruiters notice, and it can come up at interview.

A safer way to use AI

  1. Give it your real CV and the job advert. Ask it to use only experience that appears in your CV.
  2. Ask for the advert’s top two or three requirements to be matched with real examples — the structure in our UK cover letter guide.
  3. Check every sentence against your CV. Delete anything you cannot back up with a real example.
  4. Make the opening and “why this employer” part yours. These are the lines that sound most generic when AI writes them.
  5. Keep it under one page and save it as a PDF if the form asks for an upload.

Red flags to remove

  • Numbers or percentages you did not give it.
  • Job titles, employers or qualifications not in your CV.
  • Phrases like “I am thrilled to apply” or “dynamic, results-driven professional”.
