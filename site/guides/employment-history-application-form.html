How to fill in employment history on a job application form

Dates, job titles, duties, gaps and reasons for leaving: how to complete the employment history section on UK application forms.

The employment history section looks simple, but it is where many forms ask for the most detail — and where inconsistencies with your CV are spotted.

What each row usually needs

  • Employer name and address. Use the legal or commonly used name, and the address of the site you worked at.
  • Job title exactly as it was.
  • Start and end dates — usually month and year. For your current job, tick “current” or enter “present”.
  • Main duties. Three to six short points, focused on what the new role cares about.
  • Reason for leaving — keep it short and neutral (see our guide).
  • Notice period for your current job (see notice period).

Order and gaps

Most forms want your current or most recent job first. Many public sector forms ask you to account for every gap over a few months — a short line such as “travelling” or “caring for a family member” is enough (see explaining gaps).

Keep it consistent with your CV

Dates and titles on the form should match your CV and what your references will confirm. Keeping one accurate master list of your jobs makes every application faster and avoids contradictions.
