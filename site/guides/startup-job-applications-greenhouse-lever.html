Applying through Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby: tips for tech and startup jobs

What to expect on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workable application forms used by many UK tech companies and startups.

Many UK tech companies and startups use application systems such as Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workable. Their forms are shorter than NHS or Civil Service forms, but they have their own quirks.

What these forms usually ask

  • Name, email, phone (often with a separate country code), location and LinkedIn URL.
  • A CV upload — and sometimes a separate cover letter upload or text box.
  • Short custom questions: right to work, sponsorship, notice period, salary expectations, “How did you hear about us?”.
  • Sometimes one longer question, such as “Why do you want to join us?” (see our guide).
  • An optional equality or demographic survey.

Tips

  • Your CV does most of the work here, so tailor its top third to the role.
  • Check dropdowns such as phone country and location, which are easy to mis-set.
  • Keep your answers to the common questions (right to work, notice period, salary range) consistent across applications.
  • If there is a cover letter box, a short, specific note beats a generic page.
