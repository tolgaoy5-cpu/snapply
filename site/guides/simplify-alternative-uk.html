A Simplify alternative for UK job applications

Looking for a job application autofill extension that works on UK forms such as NHS Jobs, TRAC, Workday and council sites? Here is how Snapply compares and when it fits.

Several Chrome extensions fill job applications from your CV — Simplify is one of the best known. If you are applying in the UK, the forms you meet are often different: NHS Jobs and TRAC, council and university systems, Workday sites of UK employers, and small employers’ own forms.

What Snapply is built for

  • UK forms: NHS Jobs and TRAC employment and education rows, Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Teamtailor and TalentLink, plus unfamiliar forms where it recognises the fields it can.
  • UK questions: right to work, sponsorship, notice period, salary range — answered once and reused, with legal answers always confirmed by you.
  • Honest AI: cover letters and supporting statements written only from your real CV; anything it cannot back up is cut.
  • Privacy: equality, disability and legal answers and referee details stay in your browser.
  • You stay in control: it never presses submit.

Price

Snapply is free for 5 applications a month. Pro plans add unlimited applications and cover letters.
