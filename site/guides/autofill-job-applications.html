How to autofill job applications (and what to check before you submit)

The options for filling job application forms faster — browser autofill, extensions and saved answers — and the checks that stop mistakes.

Most job application forms ask for the same things: contact details, work history, education, right to work, notice period and a CV upload. Filling them by hand for every application is slow, and retyping makes mistakes more likely.

Your options

  • Browser autofill (Chrome, Safari, Edge) fills name, email, phone and address. It is free and built in, but it does not handle work history, education rows or application questions.
  • A master document you copy from. Free and reliable, but still manual.
  • Job application autofill extensions. These read your CV and fill the longer parts of the form — job history, education, upload fields and common questions. Check which application systems they support (for example Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, TRAC) and how they handle your data.

What to check, whatever you use

  1. Dates: start and end dates in the format the form wants, and your current job marked as current.
  2. Dropdowns: degree level, country and phone type are easy to get wrong.
  3. The right file in the right slot: CV in the CV upload, cover letter in the cover letter upload.
  4. Eligibility and legal questions: right to work, sponsorship, criminal record and declarations should always be answered by you, carefully.
  5. Written answers: anything that sounds generic should be rewritten for the role.

Privacy

Your CV contains personal data. Before using any tool, read where your data is stored, whether it is used to train AI models, and how to delete it.
