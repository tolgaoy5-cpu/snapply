Is there an autofill for NHS Jobs and TRAC applications?

Yes: Snapply is a Chrome extension that fills NHS Jobs and TRAC application forms from your CV — personal details, employment history, education and saved answers. You check each section and submit yourself.

Short answer: yes. Snapply is a free Chrome extension that fills NHS application forms on NHS Jobs and TRAC (apps.trac.jobs) from your CV.

What it fills

  • Personal and contact details
  • Employment history — each job as its own row, with start and end dates in the format the form accepts, and your current job marked as current
  • Education and qualifications — one row per qualification, with institution, subject, grade and date
  • Training and certificates from your CV
  • Questions you have answered before, such as notice period or right to work (legal questions are only suggested — you confirm them)

What it does not do

  • It never presses submit — you check every section first.
  • It never fills equality monitoring, disability or other personal questions on its own.
  • It never puts your details into referee or employer contact fields.

How to use it

  1. Add Snapply to Chrome and upload your CV once.
  2. Open the TRAC or NHS Jobs application and go to a section, for example Employment history.
  3. Press “Fill this page” in the Snapply panel, check the rows, then press the site’s own Save and Continue.

The supporting information box is still best written by you against the person specification — see our NHS supporting statement guide. Snapply can draft it only from your real CV, and you edit it before using it.
