How to autofill Workday job applications

Workday asks for your whole CV again on every employer’s site. Snapply fills My Information and My Experience — every job, education entry and date — from your CV in one click.

Workday is used by many large UK employers, and each one has its own careers site and account. The slow part is My Experience, where every job and qualification has to be typed into separate boxes with dates.

Options

  • Workday’s own CV upload pre-fills some fields, but it often misses or mixes up jobs and dates, so you still check every entry.
  • Browser autofill fills name, email and phone, but not the experience or education rows.
  • Snapply (Chrome extension) fills My Information and the whole My Experience page — every job and education entry, with dates Workday accepts — and attaches your CV. Questions you answered before are filled from your saved answers.

Using Snapply on Workday

  1. Upload your CV to Snapply once.
  2. On each Workday step press “Fill this page”, check the page, then press Workday’s Save and Continue.
  3. On Application Questions, answer anything new once in the Snapply panel — it is remembered for the next employer.

Snapply never submits the application and never fills the Voluntary Disclosures or Self Identify pages for you. See also our Workday guide.
