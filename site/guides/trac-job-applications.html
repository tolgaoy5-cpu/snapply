TRAC job applications: how to fill in the NHS TRAC form

A section-by-section guide to TRAC applications (apps.trac.jobs) used by many NHS trusts: employment history, education, references and supporting information.

Many NHS trusts, and some other public bodies, use TRAC for recruitment. When you press “Apply” on an advert, you are often taken to an application form on apps.trac.jobs. It is long, but it follows the same pattern every time, so it gets quicker once you know it.

Before you start

  • Have your full work history to hand, with month and year start and end dates, employer addresses and a short list of duties for each job.
  • List your qualifications with the institution, subject, grade and date.
  • Ask two referees in advance, including your current or most recent line manager (see our guide to references).
  • Read the person specification — the supporting information section is judged against it.

The main sections

  1. Personal details and contact information. Use an email address you check daily: interview invitations often arrive by email with short deadlines.
  2. Right to work and professional registration. Answer the right to work questions exactly (see our guide). If the role needs registration (for example NMC or HCPC), have your PIN and expiry date ready.
  3. Employment history. Your current or most recent job comes first, then previous jobs. Each one is a separate row with dates, employer, job title and duties. Explain gaps briefly — see explaining gaps.
  4. Education, qualifications and training. One row per qualification. Include relevant short courses and mandatory training where the form asks for it.
  5. References. Usually covering the last few years of employment or study.
  6. Supporting information. This is where shortlisting is mostly decided. Address the person specification criterion by criterion — our NHS supporting statement guide shows how.
  7. Declarations and equality monitoring. Read each declaration carefully. Equality questions are usually voluntary (see equality monitoring).

Tips that save time

  • Save each section as you go. Forms can time out, and you can come back to a saved draft.
  • Write the supporting information in a document first, then paste it in.
  • Your TRAC account keeps some details between applications, but check every section again — especially your current job and dates.
  • Adverts can close early when a trust receives many applications, so do not leave it to the closing date.
